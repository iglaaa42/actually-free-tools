Image resizer & converter

Resize, convert and compress as many images as you like. They never leave your device — there's no upload, no queue, no file-size cap and no watermark on the way out.

Format & quality

Only applies to JPEG and WebP. PNG is lossless, so quality does nothing to it.

    What this does and doesn't do

    • Nothing is uploaded. Your browser reads the file off your disk, resizes it on a canvas and hands you the result. The page makes no network requests at all — check the network tab, or turn the Wi-Fi off and watch it keep working.
    • Big downscales are done in stages. Going from 4000 px to 300 px in one step makes a noisy, aliased mess, because the browser's filter only samples a small neighbourhood. This halves repeatedly and finishes on the exact target, which is what image editors do.
    • Resizing drops metadata as a side effect. The output is drawn fresh from decoded pixels, so EXIF, GPS coordinates and camera serial numbers don't survive the trip. Rotation is applied first, so photos taken sideways come out the right way up. If you want to remove metadata without re-encoding and losing quality, use the metadata stripper instead.
    • No limits. No cap on file count, no maximum resolution beyond what your browser will allocate, no daily quota, no "upgrade for batch mode".
    • Not included: cropping, rotation, filters, background removal. Those want a real editor, and there are good free ones.

    The resize maths is plain, testable JavaScript: node tests/image.mjs.
