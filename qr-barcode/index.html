QR & barcode generator

Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no watermark, no redirect service in the middle — the code you generate encodes exactly what you typed, forever.

What this does and doesn't do

  • Nothing leaves this page. The QR and barcode encoders run locally; the page makes no network requests at all, which you can confirm in your browser's network tab (or by loading it with the Wi-Fi off — download the repository and open this page straight from disk).
  • Your QR encodes your data directly. Plenty of "free" generators hand you a code pointing at their redirect domain, so they can count scans, insert ads, or switch the destination off once a trial ends. There is no redirect here — scanning gives back exactly the text shown below the preview.
  • Print-ready output. PNG for quick use, SVG when it needs to scale to a poster or a label.
  • Not included: tracking, analytics, "dynamic" codes, logo embedding in the middle of the symbol (which trades away error correction for branding).

Every encoder in this page is readable source you can check, and the test suite verifies it against independent reference implementations: node tests/test.mjs.
